I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at [Princeton University](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/), co-advised by [Manoel Horta Ribeiro](https://manoelhortaribeiro.github.io/) and [Aleksandra Korolova](https://www.korolova.com/).

My research aims to make AI systems safe, reliable, and beneficial to society, especially in high-stakes domains like health and science. I develop interdisciplinary methods to (1) rigorously evaluate AI systems, identifying their failures and limitations across populations and building more valid measures of their capability; (2) computationally study human behavior and communication, from online communities to clinical encounters; and (3) build AI agents that synthesize evolving scientific evidence to support informed decision-making. I also develop [SciConBench](https://huggingface.co/datasets/hayoungjung/SciConBench) and its [live dashboard](https://sciconbench.cs.princeton.edu/), tracking how well the latest AI agents synthesize scientific conclusions.

Previously, I received my M.S. at the [Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science & Engineering](https://www.cs.washington.edu/) at the [University of Washington](https://www.washington.edu/), advised by [Tanushree Mitra](https://faculty.washington.edu/tmitra/). I was also fortunate to conduct research with [Yulia Tsvetkov](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~yuliats/), [David Jurgens](https://jurgens.people.si.umich.edu/), [Monojit Choudhury](https://mbzuai.ac.ae/study/faculty/monojit-choudhury/), and [Munmun De Choudhury](http://www.munmund.net/). Before that, I received undergraduate degrees in Computer Science and Political Science from the University of Washington.
